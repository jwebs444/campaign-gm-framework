# Space, awareness, groups and encounters

## Site readiness

Before dependent narration at a source-defined site, inspect actual source keys and maps. Recover the adopted local plan and parent area together. Record scale, orientation, elevations, routes, entrances, barriers, intermediate spaces and sight lines. One geometry/time model governs all actors and effects.

A source-read receipt or image generated from prose is not a geometry check. Register map references as `format: visual` with their Roll20 locator; the CLI keeps visual inspection as an explicit unresolved dependency. Source map art remains private. Generated illustrations must follow the adopted source geometry and cannot redefine it.

Use actual entrances and travel routes, including plausible turnoffs before the player names them. Preserve regional, town and local scales. Reassess dependent positions, timing and views when a premise changes. Do not preserve an accidental draft coordinate above the accepted situation.

Keep one compact applied geometry record even in theater-of-the-mind encounters: source identity, scale/units, orientation, origin, parent/exterior registration, relevant rooms/openings, elevations, solids and traversable routes. Track consequential occupied spaces, movement routes and effect footprints in that frame. Distinguish source spawn, played position, proposed correction and displayed token.

Check sight and clear projectile/spell paths separately through ALL intervening openings. Measure actual movement, bottlenecks and group footprints. Apply the adopted area/cover rules to allies and terrain too. A regional pictogram is not a scaled footprint; a scalar distance is not a complete coordinate. Broad zones suffice only when all plausible placements yield the same ruling. Resolve material uncertainty before a roll or expenditure.

Derive viewpoint art from the same map: camera/facing, horizontal distance versus elevation, proportions and occlusion. Inspect the result before presenting it; reject materially wrong geometry even if attractive. Hidden source-map details remain hidden. The adopted plan, not generated art, controls targeting.

## Movement and awareness

At meaningful changes in movement, setting, knowledge, communication or attention, consider every relevant participant, including stationary and unseen actors. Determine actual sensory access, distance, obstructions, attention, prior knowledge and available means.

Route feasibility and line of sight do not by themselves establish awareness. Apply adopted perception and stealth rules, including passive opportunities where applicable. Give perceptible cues before dependent pursuit or reports. Never turn a missed historical detection check into retroactive discovery or harm.

Track separate maps through real connections and elapsed time. A messenger, sound, spell or order must traverse its actual channel. Known resources outside current sight can matter; they are not automatically present or available.

## Groups and shared interests

Identify common objectives, actual authority, roles, contingencies, communications, cohesion and individual differences. A spokesperson needs a real mandate. Shared affiliation or silence does not establish collective knowledge or consent.

Reassess coordination when danger, terms or interests change. Characters may bargain, retreat, surrender, protect others or dissent when supported. Do not force agreement or disagreement to display complexity. Allies can coordinate without becoming unrelated turn-taking strangers; communication does not grant extra actions.

## Combat and extended approaches

Use the adopted rules and verified abilities, including action economy, concentration, ranges, cover, movement, senses and resource costs as applicable to the system. State material physical constraints before commitments. Roll uncertain outcomes with actual dice.

During initiative, reassess each actor's information and legal options after relevant events before its action. Respect readied actions and reactions without silently granting extras. Keep one initiative/time model; track who has already acted. Player choices remain the players'. Resolve routine turns only within their authorization, stopping for meaningful new choices.

Approaches across long distances need meaningful boundaries, detection opportunities, routes and time. Do not compress through a required decision. During travel use the adopted encounter schedule and caps. Stable scenery does not require random danger, and unfamiliarity does not permit bespoke threats that bypass rules.

## Visual use

Update live visual maps only when requested or actively used. Ordinary narrative movement updates records without obligatory UI synchronization. When visual use resumes, reconcile accepted positions with the display. Do not let a disputed visual update become an adjudication.

Use the least costly adequate method: cached verified maps for unchanged geometry, live inspection for current UI claims, and direct source checks for missing spatial facts. A visual should convey what the characters can perceive; it must not expose hidden tokens, locations or secrets.

## Fair corrections and applied readiness

Trace a disputed premise upstream to the earliest affected deployment, entrance, route, scale, time or player intention. Distinguish impossible events from feasible events with missing measurements. Saved but undelivered continuations remain provisional. Freeze dependent adjudication at the last delivered boundary while investigating.

Preserve unaffected actions and valid rolls. Return materially changed player choices to their players. Do not silently relocate them, add exposure/damage or manufacture retroactive detection to fit old prose. Reconsider affected NPC choices from corrected pre-action knowledge, without later rolls or plans as foreknowledge. Use the campaign's adopted repair authority before applying a rewind.

Readiness requires applied geometry PLUS separate awareness, communication and decision checks at meaningful transitions. A source read or coordinate calculation is insufficient. Demonstrate the model in the actual ruling, not a checklist recited to the player.
