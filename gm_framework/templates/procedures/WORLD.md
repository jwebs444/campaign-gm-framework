# Living world and consequential decisions

## Operations and checkpoints

Use one operations owner. Each consequential commitment needs an actor, purpose, means, status, last resolved step and next trigger. Include recipients and channel for reports. Cadence is separate from completion status. Keep a real blocker only when supported by the fiction.

Example shape (the values below illustrate structure, not campaign facts):

```json
{
  "id": "repair-pump",
  "actors": ["engineer"],
  "purpose": "Restore the district water supply",
  "means": "Tools and replacement valve already delivered",
  "cadence": "ACTIVE",
  "status": "in-progress",
  "last_step": "Fault located; old valve removed",
  "next_tick": 540,
  "before": ["pump-house", "water-status-report"],
  "condition": "Resolve when the engineer has time to fit and test the valve",
  "blocker": null,
  "delivery": "Report results to the council through the appointed runner"
}
```

Ticks are absolute fictional units configured by the campaign. The CLI matches explicit clock and destination triggers. Unfiltered attention and transitions expose the whole unfinished queue; ordinary focused preparation selects related and due work. The GM evaluates conditions, access, uncertainty, relative deadlines and unqueued promises. Retrieval does not execute work or interpret natural-language predicates.

At each due step, resolve actual progress, failure, a decision, or a supported obstacle. Update the last step and next opportunity. Keep procurement, payment, delivery, renewal and parent-project duties after a local step completes. Demote settled work; do not repeat settled consultations. Stable routines need no invented development.

## Communications and investigations

Separate information acquisition, selection, transmission, receipt and interpretation. A short initial report can omit a detail the witness already knows; later questioning can reveal its importance without inventing new surveillance.

For each consequential message establish sender, recipient, actual content, carrier/channel, departure and arrival constraints. Arrival at an intermediate location does not imply final delivery. Silence does not confirm receipt. An agent cannot act on a superior's private knowledge without communication.

Investigators follow available evidence and plausible routes. Ask what each witness knows, wants, fears and will reveal. Subtle answers and plausible deniability are legitimate. Test inference strength; a wagon, stranger, color or rumor alone may not identify cargo, a person or a purpose. Do not preserve an earlier investigation route after its premise changes.

## Important actor decisions

Before an irreversible or highly consequential action, assess the actor independently of the player's proximity:

- Current goals, emotions, self-image, values, relationships and relevant experience.
- Earned facts, attributed reports, assumptions, suspected deception and important unknowns.
- Available means, verified abilities, resources, access, timing and communication.
- Plausible options, including a simpler alternative, source-defined social options and deliberate patience.
- Expected benefit, cost, likely response, future loss of access/control, and the next useful decision point.

Choose behavior from that assessment. Explain supported mistakes through the actor's beliefs and emotions; intelligence is not omniscience or perfect rationality. Reassess who acts after new information. Do not send the same agent merely because an old draft did. Scarce information abilities need timing tied to a decision, not automatic earliest use.

Store the concise decision, decisive evidence, material tradeoff and next trigger in the existing operation/scene owner. Do not produce a private reasoning transcript as player dialogue. Give opponents counterplay and obey their actual rules and information limits.

Maintain an expandable agenda of consequential independent actors: antagonists, allies, leaders, strategists, institutions and others with relevant commitments. Prioritize by consequence, instability and opportunity. Stable actors need no full review each response. Do not collapse several individuals into one generic faction voice.

Register consequential independent actors in `review_actors`, including source-active people not yet encountered when relevant. The world phase also joins actors and governing source refs from unfinished operations. This is an agenda, not automatic knowledge or action. Reconsider means, scarce-resource timing and who should act after new reports; do not preserve an earlier itinerary through inertia.

## Source events and opportunities

Review applicable published events before their conditions are passed. Record whether each is used, adapted, deferred with a trigger, prevented, or deliberately retired. A prominent event must not disappear because the GM forgot it. Its existence does not force it to happen.

Prepare nearby routes, turnoffs, relationships, projects, discovery conditions and conditional long-term opportunities. Preserve fixed secrets and actual source geography. Do not redirect every player choice toward the same reward or relocate hidden items to meet them.

For major actors, compare source behavior with adopted characterization and current events. Explicit supplements can change motives, rules or encounters only after adoption. Cite the controlling owner and contrary constraints. Source prose alone is not evidence the character learned a fact.

Read applicable complete special-event material, including branches embedded in keys, parent/home chapters and aftermath sections. Preventing one encounter does not erase other options. A player reminder restores an omitted option to comparison; it is not necessarily a preference to enact it. Compare the strongest meaningful alternative before choosing.

Use existing operations for approaching events, with source/override refs, responsible actors/location, actual trigger and clock origin, prerequisites, cancellation conditions, preparation gaps, fictional status and next opportunity. Do not create another event schedule. Separate source reading, preparation, offered hook, acceptance, activation, off-screen outcome and delivered news.

Readiness requires actual actor knowledge, motives, means, location, communication/travel, rules/maps, perceivable lead-in and meaningful choices. Missing GM preparation is not a fictional obstacle. Reassess events after intervention, leadership change, actor loss, restored protection or retcon. Do not replay obsolete scripts, restart missed timers or disguise extra random threats to bypass encounter limits.

Pair significant successes with practical aftermath and who could notice. Reliable service, confidence, gratitude, disagreement, changed routines or simple durable benefit are valid. Let improvements remain useful; not every beneficiary, spared enemy or restoration must become another threat or errand.

## Recurring preparation catalogue

During preparation assess each category made relevant by recent play, changed premises, approaching opportunities or unresolved obligations. Apply it separately to each protagonist and significant companion. This is a selection aid, not fourteen compulsory developments.

| Category | Decision when relevant |
|---|---|
| Personalized acquisition | Does the benefit fit this person, and is its giver, maker, material, price or route feasible? Concepts, placement, promises and acquisition differ. |
| Clues/discoverability | Has witness, access, trust or location changed? Avoid accidental single-clue dependence without moving fixed secrets. |
| Player knowledge/deduction | What was delivered, inferred or remains uncertain? Do not supply player thoughts or block sound early deductions to preserve a reveal. |
| Foreshadowing/payoff | Has earlier significance become forgotten, repetitive or overtaken? Not every incidental detail is a secret. |
| Disclosure | Would trust, danger, questions or shared experience change this individual's choice to reveal or investigate? Preparation alone discloses nothing. |
| Surprise fairness | Does the twist follow from facts and preserve choices? Remove impossible knowledge and cancellation of earned success. |
| Personal/companion arcs | How do experiences affect independent interests, convictions, faith, relationships and current tensions? Players own their inner lives. |
| Consequences/callbacks | What practical or emotional effect can become observable through a real channel? No compulsory celebration or new threat. |
| Promises/projects | What step, blocker, delivery, payment or player decision remains? A promise is not fulfillment. |
| Learning/progression | Reconcile practice, finite requirements, unused accomplishment, neglected opportunities and advancement. |
| Hooks/choices | Are leads actionable and distinct? Retire obsolete problems without choosing an itinerary. |
| Challenge/readiness | Account for capabilities, allies, political gains and actual expenditure. Do not scale away advantages or force showcases. |
| Long-term direction | Which truths are settled and which design questions remain open? Resolve approaching dependencies before clues rely on them. |
| Presentation/pacing | Balance the agreed genre with discovery, danger, reward, humor, beauty, companionship and ordinary life. Avoid forced moods and spoilers. |

Conclude in the existing rolling preparation with a few meaningful opportunities: what changed, why it matters, the unresolved question, a plausible opening, whose initiative could act, and what would satisfy, alter or retire it. Keep source-event coverage and due work intact. After world resolution correct only changed dependencies.

Use existing development/operation entries to notice persistent neglect. An opportunity copied across successive substantive reviews needs a feasible opening or supported blocker, not another reminder. This never guarantees a reward or scene. Reconcile milestone pacing against the agreed campaign, retain unused credit, and avoid arbitrary delay or trivial advancement.

## Broad review and rewards

Follow the operating core's five phases in order. Track completed phase and interrupted work so catch-up does not replay outcomes. Select relevant categories rather than adding a second queue: character arcs, knowledge/clues, deduction, foreshadowing/payoff, source events, social aftermath, projects, practice, items/abilities, advancement, challenge, pacing and long-term openings.

Assess these separately for each protagonist and significant companion. Give rewards through credible opportunities and the adopted progression system. Credit established work once. Retain unused achievement credit. Announce awards and verify player-controlled build choices. An unresolved opportunity repeatedly copied without a feasible next step is preparation debt.

Finish with a few meaningful current opportunities in the existing scene preparation, not a compulsory itinerary. When an earlier review was missed, repair supported state without inventing retroactive attacks, messages, discoveries, punishment or losses. Mark uncertainty and resolve it before dependent narration.
