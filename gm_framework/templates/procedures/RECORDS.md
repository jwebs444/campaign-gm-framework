# Records, corrections and cleanup

## Accepted-record boundary

Ordinary retrieval reads accepted owners and registered original sources. Rejected branches, mixed transcripts, evaluation answers, old handoff snapshots and maintenance backups are excluded. Keep explicit historical investigations separate from play preparation. A plausible old search hit does not restore withdrawn canon.

Use coherent owners with exact heading/JSON-pointer selectors. Preserve full profiles and detailed accepted scenes once. Link concise chronology, knowledge and project results back to that evidence. Avoid competing summaries of mutable facts. Keep current location/resources in the state owner; profile history can describe earlier locations without becoming another CURRENT field.

When wording matters, retain exact terms or a faithful itemized account: who said/offered what, to whom, conditions, audience, response and unresolved duties. Presence does not imply hearing, understanding or acceptance. Preserve distinctive small remarks and gestures when they explain later humor, affection, recognition or development. Add precise locators to affected profiles/operations, not duplicate transcripts.

Correction markers alone are insufficient. Purge withdrawn prose and unsupported derived interpretations from ordinary readable owners before resuming. A broad owner read containing a registered inactive/proposed child must fail until cleaned or narrowed; discovery cannot use that path to bypass the accepted-record boundary. Preserve unrelated accepted material and necessary unresolved evidence.

## Delivered changes

Save actual narrated actions and accepted rulings. Prewritten continuations remain proposals until delivered. Reconcile the final answer before the next adjudication when a prepared draft changed.

For a resource transaction verify character, ability, cost, timing, target and actual delivery. Update resources/effects and the scene together. A hypothetical cast, level-up, old plan or abandoned draft does not spend or restore resources. Tool receipts are technical evidence, not fictional events.

`update OWNER --from-file FILE --expect HASH` supports guarded single-owner JSON replacement. It does not interpret the change, update other owners, or permit concurrent writers. Use a single writer for campaign mutations, then read back affected selectors and `audit`. Reconcile multi-owner changes before resuming play. If interrupted mid-change, keep HOLD until all affected owners agree.

## Canon corrections

Identify the accepted replacement and which events remain valid. Remove obsolete live claims and rejected scene text from ordinary retrieval. Preserve unrelated history, character depth, player actions and valid rolls.

Use `impact REF` to find declared dependent references and entities. Also inspect semantic consequences: knowledge, plans, resources, routes, reports, deadlines, source adoption, relationships and later scenes. Declared links are not exhaustive causal reasoning.

Update each affected owner in place. Add a compact correction-index entry with owner pointers and the adopted result. Keep rejected originals outside the campaign retrieval boundary if they must be retained for an explicitly requested investigation. Re-run affected preparation and recovery output before resuming. A corrected top paragraph does not clear a contradictory row elsewhere.

Reconstruction choices made to preserve accepted play are repair decisions, not future NPC tactics or guarantees that scenes remain safe. Separate that rationale from forward-play rules.

## Source provenance and adoption

Store original source fields with game/object/field identity, edition, capture time, completeness and hash. Capture time measures retrieval, not fictional age. Linked maps and other fields remain separate dependencies even when one field is complete.

Keep original text and adopted overrides distinct. A source reference has `status: source`; it does not establish that its events happened or that any character knows them. A proposed supplement remains proposed until accepted. Record applicable overrides and contrary constraints in mechanics, profiles, places or other owners, with source links.

Missing source material blocks only dependent claims. Inspect the configured Roll20 game for it. Do not fill a gap from memory while claiming source support. A route/index is a pointer, not evidence the full source was read.

## Scheduled cleanup

Execute this during the cleanup phase of every actual broad review, and immediately for adopted corrections:

1. Inspect affected live owners for contradictions, stale present-tense claims, duplicates, superseded plans and completed tasks still marked pending.
2. Preserve unique accepted dialogue, small emotional moments, causal history, unresolved correction evidence and dependencies in their owners.
3. Close completed steps and retain genuine remaining obligations. Re-evaluate tentative means against the agreed objective; do not let a sketch become a constraint.
4. Update affected routes/selectors and inspect their output. Preserve important departed characters' profile/history routes.
5. Restore the exact current scene and HOLD, including unanswered player input.

This is an integrated review phase, not an independent maintenance scheduler. Do not rewrite the whole history or run all tests on every response. Tests verify structure; actual narration must still use the records correctly.

## Privacy and handoffs

Keep secrets and private character knowledge out of player-facing documents and summaries unless requested within an appropriate GM-only context. Review linked artifacts as carefully as chat text. Exclude private source caches, transcripts, receipts and identifiers from public exports.

Handoffs freeze an as-of snapshot for an explicitly requested transfer. They do not become the current owner. New instructions do not authorize creating chats or sending messages. Preserve exact player input and live-owner paths without old rejected responses or hypothetical answer keys.
