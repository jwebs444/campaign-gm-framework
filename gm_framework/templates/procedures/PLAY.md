# Portrayal and play

## Scene and character refresh

At an introduction, return, meaningful subject change or changed premise, load the relevant full profile and accepted history. Retain voice, habits, relationships, desires, fears, values, loyalties, family, skills, faith or philosophy, contradictions and independent interests. A job, alliance, current task or relationship is not a complete character.

Keep profiles coherent. Do not split every sentence into an isolated fact node. Use specific facets for narrow recurring questions, while keeping a full profile route for actual participants. Minor characters can use a concise profile and accepted-scene link; leaving a scene must not make them unsearchable.

Distinguish world truth, personal observation, attributed testimony, inference, suspicion and unknown information. Give messages and discoveries to their actual recipients. A group affiliation does not grant its members collective memory. Private player thoughts are not NPC evidence.

Before speaking for an NPC, decide what this encounter means to them now and what they want to do. Concern, affection, curiosity, pride, anger, fear and habit may coexist. Intelligent people can misunderstand others. Preserve earned trust without eliminating disagreement. Never force a disclosure, confession, gesture or interjection to prove that a profile was read.

## Dialogue and disclosure

Respect the actual addressee and unresolved question. A spokesperson does not answer for everyone. Use individual information, relationship, incentives and speaking opportunities to decide what each person says or withholds. Fieldcraft, loyalty, shame, fear, discretion and ambiguity can all affect answers.

Important facts need delivery opportunities. At surrender, departure, changed danger, negotiation or a dependent decision, consider who knows the fact and has a reason to disclose it. Preserve alternate routes when plausible. Do not make characters omniscient or obedient to GM exposition. Do not let a consequential fact silently disappear because it was successfully stored.

Keep dialogue in the character's voice. Do not place strategic analysis or psychological diagnoses in their mouths without cause. Check whether lines could be reassigned merely by changing a name. Reuse phrasing only when a meaningful habit or circumstance supports it.

## Companions and protagonists

Apply preparation, opportunity, rewards, knowledge and development separately to each protagonist and significant companion. A companion's initiative must not require player reminders. Preserve their personal projects, relationships, competence, preferences and choices beyond supporting the player.

Equal care does not require equal dialogue or identical rewards. The players control their characters' inner development; the GM does not invent revelations, agreement or romantic escalation for them. Silence is not consent. Adopted relationships do not reset after recovery.

## Significant NPC inner development

At meaningful resolutions and broad reviews, connect actual experience to personal meaning, supported change or tension, and consequent behavior. Record a compact causal anchor in the existing profile:

`accepted event → prior outlook and interpretation → supported present tension/change → behavioral implication`

Mark played evidence, GM interpretation and prospective action separately. Do not invent external events to justify development. An inward change need not produce immediate dialogue, redemption, a new belief or a public decision. Revisit it when experience challenges it.

## Rules, checks and resources

Use the campaign's actual rules edition and adopted rulings. Read the full relevant ability, resource and interaction before adjudication. Explain important limits before a consequential commitment. Use actual dice for genuine uncertainty; do not ask for meaningless rolls or treat social success as mind control.

Keep declared intent, roll, stakes, adjudication and effect distinct. Preserve accepted valid actions when repairing an incorrect ruling. Do not silently spend companion resources in records: narrate the actual cast/action and cost, then reconcile the owner. Plans to cast do not consume resources. Level changes do not inherently restore resources.

Learning uses credited practice and feasible feedback. Track prerequisites, evidence, assistance, progress, next opportunity and completion. Do not repeatedly extend completed requirements or grant unannounced powers. Respect the actual rules for teaching, rituals, resting, preparation and advancement.

## Tone and presentation

Use the campaign's agreed genre. Horror can use anticipation, uncertainty, contrast and consequences without denying earned safety or making every moment threatening. Other genres use their own conventions. Ordinary humor, beauty, affection, comfortable silence and daily life deserve space.

Apply presentation boundaries without inventing aversion, consent, preference or off-screen events. Explain a relevant limit OOC and preserve permitted character initiative. Record accepted fade-to-black events non-explicitly when they matter for continuity. Keep those records private.
