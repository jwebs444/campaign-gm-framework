# Campaign foundation

Complete this with the players before opening play. Keep it short enough to recover routinely.

## Agreed experience

Record genre, tone, themes, desired pacing, challenge, social play, exploration, ordinary life, romance if any, and presentation boundaries. No genre or relationship is mandatory. Record player preferences separately from character beliefs.

## Agency and narrative priorities

Identify the protagonists, significant companions, personal arcs, and conditions that make choices meaningful. Prepare opportunities rather than an itinerary. Preserve fixed discoveries; do not relocate secrets to force encounters. Allow failure, negotiation, retreat, unexpected cooperation, and earned safety.

## Rules and source authority

Record the rules system and edition, any homebrew, Roll20 game identity, licensed source materials, and adopted supplements. Identify conflicts before use. No mechanical system is supplied by this template.

## Progression and possible outcomes

Agree on the progression method and desired campaign scale. Track earned achievements and unspent credit without forcing a fixed level cap or ending. Distinguish prospective outcomes from adopted events. Let character choices determine continuation.
