# Optional table rulings

These are reusable table rulings, not engine defaults or claims about a published ruleset. Adopt each explicitly in a private campaign's mechanics owner, identify its rules edition and affected characters, and link verified base rules. Initialization applies none of them. Setting lore, personal spell lists, earned character benefits, current resources and adventure exceptions are deliberately absent.

## Milestones and learning

One substantial resolved objective or breakthrough, or a coherent bundle of two or three smaller consequential achievements, can earn a level. Evaluate significance across protection, lasting change, discovery, resource recovery, personal arcs and obstacles resolved through combat, negotiation, ingenuity or avoidance. Routine errands, accepting quests and elapsed time do not independently qualify.

Count an outcome once, carry partial and surplus credit forward, and do not change requirements after completion. Independently consequential stages can qualify separately. There is no minimum time at a level or one-level-per-session limit. Set progression expectations for the chosen campaign without imposing a fixed final level, itinerary or ending. Announce earned advancement and apply verified build choices at a safe opportunity. Increased capacities do not refill expended resources, grant a rest or choose preparations retroactively.

Bonus learning supplements normal class access. Define a finite task and its exact reward: personal spell-list access, always-prepared access, an additional known spell, proficiency, expertise or a separate limited feature. These are different benefits. Specify prerequisites, credited work, feedback, independent demonstration and completion. Failed attempts with useful feedback and competent work without a roll can contribute; a single exceptional die result does not establish mastery. Do not substitute a narrower technique for an agreed full proficiency or impose new acquisition gates after completion. Apply each character's development independently.

## Inspiration variant

For a table using this reroll variant, each eligible character has one independent, nonstacking GM Inspiration charge. Spend after an attack roll, ability check or saving throw to reroll and keep the new result, even when lower. It is not advantage and is distinct from named class features. No automatic natural-20 award, session-end expiration or banked extra charge applies. Establish eligibility and award criteria in the campaign. Players choose their own spends; narrate NPC spends and actually roll before updating balances.

## Reliable routine work

Take 10 is available when safe, unthreatened and undistracted, using normal action time. Legal bonuses still require their actual timing and resources. Take 20 requires repetition, sufficient time and no consequence for failed attempts. Help requires useful actual assistance. Explain reliable approaches supported by experience and observable conditions before demanding a routine check, without exposing secrets or choosing the player's approach.

## Ritual and resource handling

When the adopted system permits it, an eligible prepared spell defaults to ritual casting when time allows unless the player chooses otherwise. State urgency before spending a slot. Verify the particular spell's eligibility, components, longer casting time and concentration; this preference grants no new ritual access. A plan, rest, capacity correction or knowledge of a spell creates no cast or active effect. Track each completed effect from its actual casting time, including elapsed duration while subsequent rituals are performed.

## Consequence horizons for divination

These are explicit adaptations for the 2014 versions of Augury and Divination, not a global adoption of another edition. Keep verified base costs, eligibility, question limits and repeat-casting reliability unless separately changed.

For Augury, the chosen course must begin or deliberately continue within thirty minutes of completion. Assess meaningful reasonably foreseeable consequences of that undertaking without a fixed consequence cutoff, including direct delayed consequences. Bound the answer by causality and the chosen scope, rather than all subsequent life choices or predetermined dice. The four omens remain omens; they do not disclose identities, directions or factual clues. A neutral result is not a guarantee of safety. Routine costs and merely imaginable mishaps do not force a mixed omen.

For Divination, ask one question about a concrete goal, event or activity occurring or genuinely beginning within seven days. The truthful brief answer can address meaningful foreseeable consequences beyond that window. Merely thinking about an unrelated distant event does not qualify it. Broader questions and informative answers distinguish it from Augury; it is not simply a longer consequence horizon. Cryptic phrasing must still answer usefully. Changed circumstances remain possible, and forecasts execute no future events.

Clarify material scope before spending, without requiring legalistic wording. Resolve from actual circumstances, credible developments and relevant sources. Missing GM reading is not a reason to manufacture a neutral answer. Keep each spell's repeated-reading accounting separate under the chosen edition. This adaptation grants no special deity relationship, interception immunity or setting-specific knowledge.

## Brief cold exposure

This optional fictional adjudication covers brief soaking followed by cold-air exposure; it is not real-world diagnostic guidance. Severe shivering disadvantages Dexterity ability checks, without automatically penalizing attacks or saves or ending concentration or consciousness. After fifteen minutes wet and exposed, use a DC 10 Constitution save against one exhaustion level; reassess further exposure every fifteen minutes with stated stakes and no retroactive rolls.

Dry insulation, wind shelter and sustained warmth stop escalation; ten minutes of effective warming clears shivering. Reaching shore or restoring hit points does not itself warm or dry someone. Incurred exhaustion uses the adopted system's recovery rules. Track actual exposure and treatment in current state and accepted history, not in this standing rule.
